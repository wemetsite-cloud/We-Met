SAFETY · UPDATED 15 AUGUST 2026

Respect and privacy come first.

We Met is restricted to adults aged 18 and older.

Protect personal information

Never share or request passwords, OTPs, UPI PINs, full bank details, private images, home addresses, personal phone numbers or off-platform contact details.

Conversation standards

Harassment, hate, coercion, explicit requests, fraud and pressure to move a conversation off-platform are prohibited. End the call and report behaviour that violates these rules.

Reporting and blocking

Members and listeners can report a conversation or account from the app. Members can block a listener from call history so they are not connected again. Administrators review reports and may restrict, suspend or close accounts. Review may include relevant call metadata and in-call text; voice audio is not recorded.

Urgent help

We Met is not an emergency or crisis service. If immediate help is needed, contact an appropriate local emergency service or a trusted person nearby.

For platform safety support, email wemetsite@gmail.com. Read our child-safety standards.
