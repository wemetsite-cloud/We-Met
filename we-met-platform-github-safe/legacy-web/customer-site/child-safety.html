CHILD-SAFETY STANDARDS · UPDATED 15 AUGUST 2026

Adults only. Zero tolerance for exploitation.

We Met is restricted to people aged 18 and older. We maintain published standards against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and act on reports promptly.

Prohibited conduct and content

We prohibit grooming, solicitation, trafficking, sexualisation or endangerment of minors, child sexual abuse material (CSAM), and any attempt to use the service to facilitate such conduct.

Reporting in the app

Members and listeners can end a conversation and submit a report from the call or call history. Reports may also be sent to wemetsite@gmail.com.

Our response

We remove prohibited content where applicable, restrict involved accounts, preserve evidence as legally required, and report apparent violations to the appropriate authorities and, where applicable, the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children.

Child-safety contact

Designated contact: wemetsite@gmail.com.
