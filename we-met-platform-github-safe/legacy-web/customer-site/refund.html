REFUND POLICY · UPDATED 15 AUGUST 2026

Clear purchase support.

Google Play purchases

Talk-time purchased in the public We Met app is processed through Google Play Billing. Google Play purchase, cancellation and refund rules apply. A verified purchase can credit talk-time only once.

Missing credit

If a completed purchase is not reflected in your wallet, contact support with the account email and Google Play order reference. We will review the purchase and wallet records.

Used talk-time

Connected talk-time that has already been consumed is ordinarily not refundable. A service failure or incorrect debit may be reviewed using call and wallet records.

Play-distributed app

The Play-distributed We Met app does not request direct bank transfer, UPI PINs, OTPs or payment screenshots.

Submit a purchase enquiry at wemetsite@gmail.com.
