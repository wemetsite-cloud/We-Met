PRIVACY POLICY · UPDATED 15 AUGUST 2026

Your information stays protected.

Information we use

We use account details, phone number, wallet transactions, call metadata, support messages, reports, listener preferences, notification delivery information and security logs to operate and protect We Met.

Calls and messages

The platform does not record call audio. In-call text messages and call metadata may be retained for service operation, safety review, dispute handling and account history. Personal phone numbers and email addresses are not shown to listeners.

Payments and listener payouts

For Google Play purchases, we process the product identifier, order information and purchase token to verify and credit talk-time. Only a one-way hash of a processed token is retained. Listener accounting records include rates, connected-call earnings, payout details, withdrawal amounts and payment references. We never request or store a UPI PIN or OTP.

Sharing and retention

Information is shared only with service providers needed to operate the platform, when required by law, or when necessary to protect users and the service. Records are kept only as long as needed for operations, security, disputes and legal obligations.

Your choices

Members can delete their account in the Android app or at the account-deletion page. Listener identity is anonymised on deletion while de-identified accounting records may be retained where legally required. For access or correction, email wemetsite@gmail.com.
