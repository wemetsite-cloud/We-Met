SAFETY · UPDATED 24 AUGUST 2026

Respect and privacy come first.

Protect personal information

Never share or request passwords, OTPs, UPI PINs, bank credentials, home addresses, personal phone numbers, private social handles or off-platform contact details. Customer and listener phone numbers are private by design.

Posts and private messages

Exclusive access never creates permission to request explicit, coercive or invasive content. Do not download, republish or distribute a listener’s exclusive post. Keep direct messages respectful and report abuse.

Conversation standards

Harassment, threats, hate, coercion, explicit requests, fraud and pressure to move a conversation off-platform are prohibited. End a call or conversation that makes you uncomfortable.

Reporting

Customers and listeners can report an account from a call or call history. Administrators can review reports, restrict access, moderate listener posts and examine relevant call metadata and text chat.

Urgent help

We Met is not an emergency, medical or crisis service. If anyone may be in immediate danger, contact an appropriate local emergency service or a trusted person nearby.

For platform safety support, email wemetsite@gmail.com.
