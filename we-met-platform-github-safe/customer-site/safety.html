SAFETY

Respect and privacy come first.

Protect personal information

Never share or request passwords, OTPs, UPI PINs, full bank details, private images, home addresses, personal phone numbers or off-platform contact details.

Conversation standards

Harassment, threats, hate, coercion, explicit requests, fraud and pressure to move the conversation off-platform are prohibited. End the call and report behaviour that makes you feel unsafe.

Reporting

Customers and listeners can report an account from a call or call history. Administrators can review reports, suspend access and examine relevant call metadata and text chat.

Urgent help

We Met is not an emergency or crisis service. If you or someone else may be in immediate danger, contact an appropriate local emergency service or a trusted person nearby.

For platform safety support, email wemetsite@gmail.com.
