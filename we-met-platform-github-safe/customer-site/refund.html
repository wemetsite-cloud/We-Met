REFUND POLICY · UPDATED 8 AUGUST 2026

Payment review without guesswork.

UPI verification

Direct UPI payments remain pending until the administrator verifies the unique transaction reference, screenshot, amount and selected plan. An approved payment credits talk-time once.

Duplicate or incorrect payments

Contact support promptly with the account email, transaction reference, payment date and amount. We will review duplicate credits, incorrect amounts and technical failures using the available records.

Used talk-time

Connected talk-time that has already been consumed is ordinarily not refundable. A service failure or incorrect debit may be reviewed using call and wallet records.

Declined submissions

A declined submission does not credit talk-time. If money left your account, contact support with the transaction record so the payment can be investigated.

Submit a payment enquiry at wemetsite@gmail.com or through the signed-in Support tab.
