Refund & Cancellation Policy

When a We Met payment may be refunded or cancelled and how to request support.

Last updated: 8 August 2026

Payment failures and cancellations

A cancelled or uncompleted checkout does not create talk-time. Failed, declined, incomplete or duplicate payment attempts do not receive wallet credit.

When a refund may be eligible

Contact support if a successful payment was duplicated, was not credited after verification, or if a confirmed technical or billing error prevented delivery of the purchased talk-time. We Met will review the payment reference and wallet records.

Used talk-time

Talk-time already consumed through a connected call is normally non-refundable because the digital service has been delivered, except where applicable law requires otherwise or We Met confirms a billing/service error.

How to request a refund

Email wemetsite@gmail.com with your account email, payment date, amount and transaction/reference ID. Do not send your PIN, CVV or OTP.

Refund timing

If a refund is approved, We Met aims to initiate it to the original payment method within 5 business days where possible. The bank or payment provider may take additional time to show the credit.

This policy does not limit any refund or consumer right that cannot lawfully be excluded.
