CHILD SAFETY · UPDATED 24 AUGUST 2026

We Met is for adults only.

Customers and listeners must be at least 18 years old. Accounts, listener applications, posts, messages or calls involving minors are not permitted.

Zero-tolerance standards

We prohibit content or conduct that exploits, endangers, solicits, grooms or sexualizes minors, and we prohibit child sexual abuse material. Relevant accounts may be restricted immediately and reports may be escalated when required by law.

Reporting

Use the in-service report controls where available, or contact wemetsite@gmail.com with enough information for the safety team to investigate. Do not resend prohibited media in a report.

Safety contact

Child-safety concerns can be sent to wemetsite@gmail.com.
