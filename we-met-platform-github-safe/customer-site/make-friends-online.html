FRIENDLY CHATS AND LISTENERS

Looking for friendly conversations online?

People search for online friends, a new person to talk to, or a voice chat that feels more natural than texting. On We Met, you can start a respectful conversation with an available listener by audio call.

A place for conversation, not guaranteed friendship

We Met connects adult customers with approved listeners. Your conversation can be casual and friendly, but it is a paid listening service: the platform does not promise personal friendship, a romantic relationship or contact outside We Met. Check the visible listener profile and language before requesting a call.

Speak, do not only type

Some people prefer a live voice chat over a long text conversation. We Met's core service is an audio call in the browser. You can use the same site on a desktop computer, laptop or smartphone and don't need to install a separate calling app.

Malayalam and other languages

Malayalam listeners and multilingual options are supported, subject to who is currently available. The public experience explains the features, while the signed-in listener directory shows actual profiles and status. Read the Malayalam voice chat guide for language-specific details.

Respectful conversations

Our community rules prohibit harassment, explicit requests and sharing private contact details. Calls use prepaid talk-time and are charged only for connected seconds. If you are comparing friendship apps, review our service differences so you know exactly what We Met offers.

Ready for a real voice conversation?

Open We Met, choose an available listener and call from your browser. No separate app is needed. Prepaid talk-time applies.
