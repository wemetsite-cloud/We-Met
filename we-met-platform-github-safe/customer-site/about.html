ABOUT WE MET

Conversation with care.

We Met is a browser-based voice conversation platform. Customers choose an available listener, communicate by voice and text during a private session, and pay only for connected talk-time.

What we provide

  • Language-aware listener discovery.
  • Private browser calling without exposing personal phone numbers.
  • Connected-second wallet billing with clear call history.
  • Customer support, reporting and administrator review tools.

What we do not provide

We Met is not an emergency service, medical service, professional counselling service, or substitute for qualified advice. Audio is not recorded by the platform.
