ABOUT WE MET

Conversation with care.

We Met is a phone-first browser conversation platform where customers discover verified listeners and connect inside a private, controlled space.

What we provide

  • Verified listener profiles with live availability, public usernames, profile photos and banners.
  • Separate ₹399 Exclusive memberships for listener posts and direct messages.
  • Private browser calls funded separately from a prepaid talk-time wallet and billed by connected second.
  • Follower controls, support, safety reporting and administrator review tools.

Listener trust

New listeners verify their phone, record an administrator-set Malayalam line and wait for approval before their profile can go live.

What we do not provide

We Met is not an emergency service, medical service, professional counselling service or substitute for qualified advice. Live call audio is not recorded by the platform.
