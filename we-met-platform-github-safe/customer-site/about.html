Last updated: 8 August 2026

About the service

We Met is an online conversation platform for adults aged 18 or above. Customers purchase prepaid talk-time and use it for one-to-one browser audio calls and in-session text chat with available Malayalam listeners.

What the customer receives

The product sold is digital talk-time. There is no physical shipment. The wallet is credited after the applicable payment verification step, and connected call time is deducted from the wallet while a live audio connection is active.

What We Met is not

We Met does not arrange offline meetings and does not provide escort, sexual, medical, counselling or emergency services. Sexual requests, harassment, fraud and attempts to obtain passwords, OTPs or banking credentials are prohibited.
