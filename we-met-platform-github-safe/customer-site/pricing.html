Pricing

Exclusive access and voice talk-time stay separate, so every charge is clear.

Last updated: 24 August 2026

Exclusive listener membership

Exclusive costs ₹399 per billing cycle for one selected listener. It unlocks only that listener’s private posts and direct messages. Subscribing to another listener requires another membership.

Calls use wallet talk-time

Calling does not require a membership. Random calls and direct listener calls start whenever the customer has talk-time and the listener is online. The wallet reduces by connected second and the call ends at zero.

Talk-time packs

Current wallet packs are loaded from the server and shown with included minutes, exact price and a Buy talk-time button before secure Razorpay checkout.

Renewal and cancellation

Listener memberships renew through Razorpay until renewal is turned off. Access continues until the current paid period ends. The customer’s Exclusive area shows each listener membership separately only after verified payment.
