Pricing

How We Met talk-time packs, checkout amounts and connected-time billing work.

Last updated: 8 August 2026

Pricing model

We Met uses prepaid talk-time. Current pack prices are loaded from the server on the main We Met page and are shown again before checkout. There is no recurring subscription unless We Met explicitly introduces one in the future with separate consent.

Billing

Wallet balance is reduced based on connected call time. Billing begins only after the live audio connection is established and stops when the connected call ends.

Before you pay

Review the selected pack, included minutes and exact payable amount. Do not complete a payment if the payee or amount does not match the checkout instructions.

Taxes and fees

Any applicable taxes or payment charges will be disclosed where required. The amount shown at checkout is the amount the customer is asked to pay for that order.
