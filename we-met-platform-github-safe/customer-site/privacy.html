PRIVACY POLICY · UPDATED 24 AUGUST 2026

Your private space stays protected.

Information we use

We use verified phone numbers, names, profile images, listener public usernames and banners, talk-time activity, call metadata, support messages, reports, notification subscriptions and security logs to operate and protect We Met. A listener’s original name and phone remain private from customers.

Voice verification

Listener applicants may provide a voice-verification recording for account review. It is not published to customers.

Posts, follows and messages

Listener photos and direct messages are stored to provide private access. Only customers with valid access for that listener can open private posts or exchange new direct messages. Customer phone numbers are never shown to listeners.

Calls

We Met does not record call audio. In-call text messages and call metadata may be retained for service operation, safety review and account history.

No online checkout

The website does not currently collect online purchase information or connect to an online checkout provider.

Your choices

You may request access or correction through Support. Customer accounts can be permanently deleted from the Profile tab or at Delete account. Some records may be retained where required for security or legal obligations.
