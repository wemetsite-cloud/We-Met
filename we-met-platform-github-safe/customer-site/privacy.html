PRIVACY POLICY · UPDATED 23 AUGUST 2026

Your information stays protected.

Information we use

We use account details, phone number, wallet transactions, call metadata, support messages, reports, listener preferences, browser notification subscriptions and security logs to operate and protect We Met.

Calls and messages

The platform does not record call audio. In-call text messages and call metadata may be retained for service operation, safety review, dispute handling and account history. Personal phone numbers and email addresses are not shown to listeners.

Payments and listener earnings

For Razorpay checkout, we store the order and payment references, selected plan, amount and verification status. We do not store card or UPI credentials. For listener earnings, we store the listener’s rate, connected-call earnings, administrator wallet adjustments and manually recorded payment references. We never request or store a UPI PIN or OTP.

Sharing and retention

Information is shared only with service providers needed to operate the platform, when required by law, or when necessary to protect users and the service. Records are kept only as long as needed for operations, security, disputes and legal obligations.

Your choices

You may request access or correction through Support. Customer accounts can be permanently deleted from the Profile tab or at Delete account. Some information may still be retained where required for security, payment disputes or legal obligations.
