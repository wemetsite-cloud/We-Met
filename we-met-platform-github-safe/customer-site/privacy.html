PRIVACY POLICY · UPDATED 24 AUGUST 2026

Your private space stays protected.

Information we use

We use verified phone numbers, names, profile images, listener public usernames and banners, talk-time transactions, call metadata, support messages, reports, notification subscriptions and security logs to operate and protect We Met. A listener’s original name and phone remain private from customers.

Voice verification

Listener applicants record the Malayalam line configured by the administrator. That recording is stored for administrator review and account-verification history. It is not published to customers.

Posts, follows and messages

Listener photos and direct messages are stored to provide Exclusive access. Only customers with an active membership for that listener can open their posts or exchange new direct messages. Listeners can see their followers and follower count; customers do not see a listener’s follower count. Customer phone numbers are never shown to listeners.

Calls

We Met does not record call audio. In-call text messages and call metadata may be retained for service operation, safety review, dispute handling and account history.

Payments and earnings

For Razorpay wallet top-ups and recurring listener memberships, we store order, subscription and payment references, selected plan or listener, amount and verification status. We do not store card details, UPI credentials, UPI PINs or payment OTPs. Listener earnings records include connected-call credits and ₹50 credits for successful membership payments.

Your choices

You may request access or correction through Support. Customer accounts can be permanently deleted from the Profile tab or at Delete account. Active recurring memberships are cancelled before deletion. Some records may be retained where required for security, disputes or legal obligations.
