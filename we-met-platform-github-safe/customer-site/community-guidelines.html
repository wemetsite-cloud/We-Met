Community Guidelines

Rules for respectful and safe use of We Met.

Last updated: 8 August 2026

Respectful adult conversations

We Met is for adults aged 18 or above. Treat listeners and customers respectfully. Consent and boundaries apply to every interaction.

Prohibited conduct

  • Sexual services, sexual requests or sexually explicit conduct.
  • Harassment, threats, hate, stalking or intimidation.
  • Requests for phone numbers, private social handles, passwords, OTPs, banking credentials or private images.
  • Fraud, impersonation, payment manipulation or attempts to move paid service transactions off-platform.
  • Illegal content or instructions that facilitate illegal activity.

Reporting

End a conversation that feels unsafe and use the report option or email wemetsite@gmail.com. We Met may review relevant account, call-status and chat records and may restrict accounts when necessary.

Emergency limitation

Listeners are not emergency responders, doctors or counsellors. Do not use We Met as an emergency or crisis service.
