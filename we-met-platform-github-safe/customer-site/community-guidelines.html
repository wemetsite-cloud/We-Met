Community Guidelines

Rules for respectful and safe use of We Met.

Last updated: 24 August 2026

Respectful conversations

Use requires meeting the minimum eligible age stated in the Terms. Treat listeners and customers respectfully. Personal boundaries apply to calls, direct messages, profiles and exclusive posts.

Prohibited conduct

  • Explicit, exploitative or coercive requests, posts, messages or conduct.
  • Harassment, threats, hate, stalking or intimidation.
  • Requests for phone numbers, private social handles, passwords, OTPs, banking credentials or private images.
  • Copying, republishing or distributing a listener’s exclusive content without permission.
  • Fraud, impersonation, payment manipulation or attempts to move paid service transactions off-platform.
  • Illegal content or instructions that facilitate illegal activity.

Reporting

End a conversation that feels unsafe and use the report option or email wemetsite@gmail.com. We Met may review relevant account, post, call-status and chat records and may restrict accounts or remove posts when necessary.

Emergency limitation

Listeners are not emergency responders, doctors or counsellors. Do not use We Met as an emergency or crisis service.
