Service Delivery

We Met delivers access digitally. No physical shipping applies.

Last updated: 24 August 2026

Talk-time delivery

Wallet minutes are credited after the We Met server verifies Razorpay’s payment signature. They can be used for random calls or direct calls to any available verified listener; no membership is required.

Membership delivery

After Razorpay confirms an active ₹399 listener subscription, that listener’s exclusive photos and direct messages unlock. Each listener membership is delivered separately and appears in the customer’s Exclusive area only after a verified payment.

Calls remain wallet-paid

Membership does not supply call minutes and is never required to place a call. Billing begins only after live audio connects and stops when the call ends or the wallet reaches zero.

No shipping

We Met sells a digital conversation service. No courier, physical delivery or shipping charge applies.
