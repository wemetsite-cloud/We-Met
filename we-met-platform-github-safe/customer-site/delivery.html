Service Delivery

We Met delivers access digitally. No physical shipping applies.

Last updated: 24 August 2026

Talk-time delivery

Wallet minutes are credited after the We Met server verifies Razorpay’s payment signature. The wallet is then available for paid browser calls when an eligible subscribed listener is online.

Membership delivery

After Razorpay confirms an active ₹399 listener subscription, that listener’s exclusive photos, direct messages and call button unlock. Each listener membership is delivered separately and appears in the customer’s Exclusive area.

No free calls

Membership unlocks the ability to call; it does not supply call minutes. The main wallet must still contain enough talk-time and billing begins only after live audio connects.

No shipping

We Met sells a digital conversation service. No courier, physical delivery or shipping charge applies.
