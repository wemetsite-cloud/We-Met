Service Delivery

How We Met delivers purchased talk-time and why no physical shipping applies.

Last updated: 8 August 2026

Digital delivery only

We Met sells a digital conversation service. No physical goods are shipped, so no courier or shipping charge applies.

Gateway payments

When an integrated payment gateway is enabled, talk-time is credited after the gateway confirms a successful captured payment.

Direct UPI verification

When direct UPI is enabled, the customer submits the successful UTR/reference and payment proof. Talk-time is credited after the administrator verifies the payment against the receiving account. We aim to review valid submissions promptly, normally within one business day.

Using delivered talk-time

Credited talk-time remains in the customer wallet for use when a listener is available. Billing starts only after the audio connection is live.
