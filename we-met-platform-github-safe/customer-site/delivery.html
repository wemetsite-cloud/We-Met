Service Delivery

How We Met delivers purchased talk-time and why no physical shipping applies.

Last updated: 23 August 2026

Digital delivery only

We Met sells a digital conversation service. No physical goods are shipped, so no courier or shipping charge applies.

Razorpay checkout

Talk-time is credited only after Razorpay returns the payment and the We Met server verifies its cryptographic signature. Manual UPI proof uploads are not accepted.

Using delivered talk-time

Credited talk-time remains in the customer’s talk-time balance for use when a listener is available. Billing starts only after the audio connection is live.
