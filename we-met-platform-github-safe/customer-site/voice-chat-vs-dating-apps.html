Voice chat websites and dating apps are different

If you searched for a dating app, Omegle alternative or FRND-style voice chat, it helps to know what We Met does—and does not—offer before you join.

What We Met does

We Met is an independent 18+ voice conversation platform. You can browse approved listeners, review their profile and available language, then request a private audio call using prepaid talk-time. Calls work through the website on a phone, laptop or desktop with a supported browser and microphone.

Is We Met a dating app?

No. We Met is not a dating, relationship matching, girlfriend-finding, escort or offline meeting service. Matching and romantic relationships are not the service being sold. Listener calls are governed by the terms and community guidelines.

Is We Met the same as FRND?

No. FRND is a separate brand and service. People searching for voice chat apps like FRND can consider whether they prefer a website-based, prepaid listener conversation. We Met is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to FRND.

Is We Met like Omegle?

Not exactly. Searchers looking for Omegle alternatives often want to meet new people online. We Met offers audio-only conversations with approved listeners, rather than an anonymous video chat roulette. It is not Omegle and is not affiliated with it.

Can I call from my computer?

Yes. Browser audio calls can be made from compatible desktops and laptops with microphone permission enabled. See how desktop voice calls work and how prepaid wallet minutes are charged.

Ready for a real voice conversation?

Open We Met, choose an available listener and call from your browser. No separate app is needed. Prepaid talk-time applies.
