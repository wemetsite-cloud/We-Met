How a Malayalam voice call works

  1. Open the official We Met website and create or sign in to an adult account.
  2. Review approved listener profiles. Select Malayalam where available, or explore other languages.
  3. Check the displayed talk-time pack and add prepaid minutes to your wallet.
  4. Choose an online listener, allow microphone access and begin the browser call once connected.

Privacy, safety and cost

Your phone number is not displayed to the listener. Calls are billed from prepaid talk-time by connected time. Listener availability and audio quality vary, and an Exclusive membership is not required to make a call. Learn more in pricing and safety.
