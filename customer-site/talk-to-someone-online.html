REAL-TIME VOICE CONVERSATION

Talk to someone online—not just a chat box

Sometimes you want an ordinary, friendly conversation rather than more scrolling or texting. We Met lets adults browse listener profiles and request an audio conversation without installing a phone app.

Choose the listener, not a random phone number

The We Met directory shows approved listener profiles and the languages they support. You can see which listeners are online and choose whom to request. There is also an availability-based connect option for customers with talk-time. The other person must be available and accept the call before you can have a live conversation.

Browser calls on your own device

Open wemetchat.com from a supported mobile or desktop browser. A microphone and internet connection are required. The voice connection uses in-browser calling; there is no need to download a separate desktop program or mobile application.

What can you talk about?

Everyday topics, hobbies, shared interests, and friendly conversations are welcome within the platform rules. Respect a listener's boundaries and do not request private contact details, explicit content or an offline meeting. We Met is a communication service, not a substitute for professional counselling or emergency help.

Understand your minutes

We Met uses prepaid talk-time. You can review available packs before checkout. Billing begins once the audio call is connected and stops when it ends; no paid subscription is required just to make a call. Read how wallet pricing works before getting started.

Ready for a real voice conversation?

Open We Met, choose an available listener and call from your browser. No separate app is needed. Prepaid talk-time applies.
